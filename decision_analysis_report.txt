PART 1 -- DECISION TIMELINE (baseline scenario)
================================================================================

Starting state:
  info_quality            0.50
  adaptability            0.55
  authority_concentration 0.55
  alignment               0.55
  constraint_capacity     0.50
  environment_pressure    0.45


[T1] DECISION -- Authority structure
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Chosen:   Centralize
  Rejected: Decentralize

  Why: EV(Centralize) = 67.77  vs  EV(Decentralize) = 61.35
  Single unified command cuts coordination noise when info quality is moderate
  and alignment is the current bottleneck. The bottleneck urgency model gives
  high marginal value to fixing the scarce factor first.

  State changes after this step:
    auth_concentration  0.55  -->  0.95  (+0.40)
    alignment           0.55  -->  0.73  (+0.18)
    info_quality        0.50  -->  0.62  (+0.12)
    constraint_capacity 0.50  -->  0.68  (+0.18)
    adaptability        0.55  -->  0.43  (-0.12)  [trade-off: slower decisions]


[T2] DECISION -- Information strategy
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Chosen:   Full transparency
  Rejected: Restrict information

  Why: EV(Full transparency) = 67.77  vs  EV(Restrict information) = 65.04
  Info quality is still moderate (0.62, below the flip threshold of ~0.65).
  The +0.30 info boost has higher marginal value than the pressure reduction
  from restricting. This choice FLIPS in scenarios 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 12-14, 16, 17.

  State changes after this step:
    info_quality         0.62  -->  0.92  (+0.30)
    alignment            0.73  -->  0.83  (+0.10)
    environment_pressure 0.45  -->  0.57  (+0.12)  [cost: bad news travels fast]


[T3] CHANCE -- Market reaction
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Most likely outcome: Favorable  (p = 0.60)
  Alternative:         Adverse    (p = 0.40)

  EV is probability-weighted:  0.60 * 73.99  +  0.40 * 58.45  =  67.77

  Favorable branch state changes:
    environment_pressure 0.57  -->  0.35  (-0.22)
    alignment            0.83  -->  0.89  (+0.06)

  Adverse branch state changes:
    environment_pressure 0.57  -->  0.85  (+0.28)
    alignment            0.83  -->  0.73  (-0.10)
    adaptability         0.43  -->  0.38  (-0.05)


[T4] DECISION -- Response mode  (on Favorable branch)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Chosen:   AI-led
  Rejected: Hybrid (71.38), Human-led (68.54)

  Why: Adaptability is the remaining bottleneck (0.43, scarce).
  AI-led gives the largest adaptability boost (+0.26). Human-led gives bigger
  alignment gain but alignment is no longer scarce at this point (0.89).

  State changes after this step:
    adaptability   0.43  -->  0.69  (+0.26)  [largest marginal gain]
    info_quality   0.92  -->  1.00  (+0.08, capped at 1.0)
    alignment      0.89  -->  0.94  (+0.05)

  (* = Info strategy flipped from baseline "Full transparency")
================================================================================

  Authority and Response mode are STABLE across all 17 scenarios:
    Authority: always  Centralize
    Response:  always  AI-led

  Only the Information strategy flips. Flip boundary:
    - info_quality >= 0.65  --> Restrict information
    - env_pressure >= 0.70  --> Restrict information
    - alignment    >= 0.70  --> Restrict information  (or <= 0.25)
    - diagonal: info up + pressure down >= 0.10  --> Restrict information

  Reasoning: when the factor being improved (info, pressure, alignment) is
  already ample, panic control (Restrict) has higher marginal value than
  providing more of an already-sufficient resource (Full transparency).

================================================================================
  FRAMEWORK INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

  Authority structure (x_str):
    Centralize is dominant in all scenarios because it simultaneously raises
    auth_concentration, alignment, info_quality, and constraint_capacity.
    The only cost (lower adaptability) is recovered by choosing AI-led response.

  Information strategy (x_inf -- limiting influence):
    This is the sensitive lever. It flips based on which factor is the binding
    bottleneck at the time the decision is made (post-Centralize):
    - If info is scarce (<0.65): pay the pressure cost for the info boost.
    - If info is ample (>=0.65): use panic control (Restrict) instead.
    - High pressure already: Restrict to stop the bleed first.
    - High alignment already: Restrict (alignment is not scarce, panic control wins).
    - Very low alignment (0.25): Restrict information to prevent rumor cascades
      that erode already-fragile alignment further.

  Response mode (x_align):
    AI-led is dominant because adaptability is always the remaining bottleneck
    after Centralize (which costs -0.12 adapt). The +0.26 adaptability boost
    from AI-led has the highest urgency multiplier in every scenario tested.

  Study dimension trade-off:
    Power distribution is always low (0.05) because Centralize pushes
    auth_concentration to 0.95. This is the cost of the authority structure
    choice: decision quality and coordination improve, but power distribution
    and ethical stability take a hit. RQ3 confirmed: concentration trades
    speed/quality for distribution/ethics.
